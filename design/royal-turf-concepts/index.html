A constant across every direction

Seven colors. Seven identities.

White, black, pink, blue, yellow, green and orange. A proposed enamel palette with a numbered badge on every horse and matching card bay.

Keep the body color visible from above; reserve metal for tack, base edges and small accents. Yellow and orange must remain distinct. Numbers and repeated identity marks support recognition beyond color. Player betting chips need their own owner colors or patterns, separate from these horse identities.

Board & betting

One shared track, with seven horse-specific betting/card bays. Keep the track cells and horse numbers readable from the overhead camera. Basic player bets use 1 / 1 / 2 chips; the hidden-bet variant adds a 0 bluff.

Card families

The edition has 21 base profiles and 28 historical profiles. Seven base profiles and eight historical movement profiles have been transcribed from photographs. A design system can serve the whole deck without inventing the missing data.

Edition accuracy

The Chinese payout photograph gives 150 for third place with three betting units. Finish/lap convention and several variant and settlement details remain unresolved. Keep these rules separate from material and styling decisions.
